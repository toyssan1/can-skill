The response advises against the SN65HVD230 for 2 Mbit/s CAN FD and recommends a specific CAN FD rated transceiver that supports 3.3 V logic through a VIO pin (such as TJA1051T/3, TJA1044GT/3, TJA1042T/3, TCAN1042V, TCAN1051V, TCAN1044V, MCP2562FD, ATA6561 or ATA6563) or a 3.3 V CAN FD part (TCAN33xG).
